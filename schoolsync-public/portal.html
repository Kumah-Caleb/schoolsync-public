How to Use This System

  • Headmasters: Activate your school with the code from your administrator, then add teachers.
  • Teachers: Login with credentials from your headmaster. Create programmes, enroll students, and record marks.
  • Students: Register with your school name to get a Student ID. Login to enroll and view grades.
  • Administrators: Use the programmer dashboard (separate URL) to register schools and generate activation codes.
